A fast, clean New Tab page that organizes your shortcuts by Firefox Multi-Account Containers.

### What it does
- **Container Swimlanes:** Groups your bookmarks and shortcuts into color-coded container lanes (Work, Personal, Banking, etc.).
- **1-Click Launch:** Opens links directly in their assigned container. Left-click for foreground, middle-click for background.
- **Native Firefox Look:** Crafted to match Firefox's modern Acorn design with soft rounded corners, refined elevation, and seamless light and dark theme adaptation.
- **Instant Filter (`/`):** Press `/` anywhere on the page to search shortcuts across all containers.
- **Favicons & Clean Avatars:** Displays real site favicons captured locally during browsing, with clean letter avatars as offline fallback.
- **Easy Customization:** Drag to reorder, add/edit links, or toggle light and dark themes.

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- `/`: Focus search filter
- `Escape`: Clear search filter

### Privacy First
- Runs 100% locally in your browser.
- Zero network requests, zero telemetry, zero analytics.

### Permissions
- `contextualIdentities` & `cookies`: Read your container names/colors and open tabs in the right container.
- `storage`: Save your shortcut layout locally on your machine.
- `tabs`: Open tabs when you click a shortcut.
